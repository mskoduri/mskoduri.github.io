Frequently Asked Questions

View All FAQs →
What is a digital invitation?

A digital invitation is an interactive invitation that guests can open on their phone through a shareable link.

Do guests need an app?

No. Guests can open the invitation in a normal mobile browser.

Can I share it on WhatsApp?

Yes. Your invitation is designed to be shared easily through WhatsApp and other messaging apps.

Can I have multiple events?

Yes, depending on the package. Wedding packages can include functions such as Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet and the main ceremony.

Can I add photos?

Yes. You can provide your selected photos for the invitation gallery.

Can guests RSVP?

Yes, RSVP can be included so you can collect responses from your guests.

How long does it take?

Delivery time depends on the design, content and level of personalisation required.

Can you create custom designs?

Yes, custom design work can be discussed for Signature or custom requirements.
